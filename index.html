About Us

Welcome to a World of Wonder

✨ A Place Where Ideas Are Celebrated

Welcome to MapleSprout Daycare, a deeply nurturing early learning center located in the heart of Maple Ridge. Imagine a place where your child’s ideas are celebrated, their curiosity is nurtured, and their daily discoveries are treated like magic. That is the environment we strive to create every single day—a safe, welcoming place where children feel confident to grow and discover the world at their own beautiful pace.

🏡 A Cozy Space for Early Learning

Step into a beautiful, bright space where your child can truly thrive! We provide a warm, nurturing environment filled with high-quality toys and natural light, making it the perfect place for your little ones to explore, learn, and play every single day.

🌱 Our Philosophy

Nurturing the “Hundred Languages”

“The child has a hundred languages, a hundred hands, a hundred thoughts, a hundred ways of thinking, of playing, of speaking.” — Loris Malaguzzi, Founder of the Reggio Emilia Approach

Our curriculum is deeply inspired by the Reggio Emilia philosophy and guided by the BC Early Learning Framework. We don’t just teach children; we learn alongside them.

By observing and listening to the children, our educators create an “emergent curriculum” that follows their natural interests. Whether they are fascinated by a rainstorm or building a city out of blocks, we turn their everyday curiosities into meaningful projects.

🍼 Infant & Toddler (I.T.)

Nurturing First Steps

Focusing on Well-being & Belonging

Your child’s first experiences away from home should feel like an extension of your family. In our I.T. program, we focus on creating a deeply nurturing and responsive environment.

  • A Calming Space: We use soft lighting, cozy corners, and safe, open spaces that encourage crawlers and early walkers to explore with confidence.
  • Respecting the Capable Child: We pay close attention to your child’s non-verbal cues and provide tactile materials (like fabric, wood, and water) that allow them to safely investigate the world using all their senses.
  • Gentle Rhythms: We follow a gentle rhythm that respects your toddler’s natural needs for rest, nourishment, and active play.
🎨 3-5 Program

Fostering Creative Minds

Focusing on Exploration, Creativity & Social Responsibility

As children grow, their natural curiosity expands into active inquiry and collaborative play. We guide children as they navigate friendships, express complex ideas, and view themselves as active members of their community.

  • Project-Based Learning: Instead of pre-planned worksheets, our curriculum emerges from what the children want to know, making learning deeply engaging.
  • Endless Expression: We provide a rich variety of open-ended materials—paint, wire, light tables, and clay—ensuring children have endless ways to express their understanding of the world.
  • Making Learning Visible: We carefully document their conversations and artwork, keeping you connected to their daily triumphs.
